Hi! I'm a fourth-year PhD student in Financial Economics at the University of Chicago Booth
School of Business. I work on financial intermediation, corporate finance, and public finance.

Before starting the PhD I was a pre-doctoral research professional at the Fama-Miller Center for
Research in Finance at Booth, and before that a research associate at CAFRAL at the Reserve Bank
of India. I hold an MSc in Economics from the London School of Economics and a BSc in Economics
from Presidency University, Kolkata.
